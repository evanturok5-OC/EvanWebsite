About the host

Meet the person behind 'The Silver Screen Spin' and 'Turok's Takes'

Evan Turok is a Film reviewer, writer and Radio DJ from Romsey Victoria. Between his radio show 'The Silver Screen Spin', playing every Monday from 6–7 pm, and his monthly written reviews in the 'Star Weekly', released state wide the second Tuesday of the month, titled under 'Turoks Takes', he has become a consistent and trusted voice in the film world.

Listen to The Silver Screen Spin on 99.3FM and read Evan's monthly reviews in Star Weekly.
